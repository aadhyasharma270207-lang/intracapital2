PATENT SPECIFICATION DOCUMENT
Patent ID: US-984512-B2
Title: Multi-Sensor Differential Thermal Analysis for Enclosed Freight & Machinery
Assignee: Intracapital Corp
Filing Date: 2024-11-14

ABSTRACT:
A system and method for predictive thermal gradient estimation in enclosed cargo containers and high-load industrial machinery. The invention utilizes differential temperature measurements between internal sensor arrays and external environmental reference nodes to compute real-time thermal flux curves.

CLAIMS:
1. An automated method for predicting thermal failure in enclosed refrigerated spaces comprising receiving multi-node wireless telemetry, calculating a thermal gradient delta rate, and issuing an early warning trigger prior to threshold breach.
2. The method of claim 1, further comprising applying machine learning pattern matching against stored historical thermodynamic logs.
3. The method of claim 1, integrated into vehicular telematics hardware via CAN bus protocol.
