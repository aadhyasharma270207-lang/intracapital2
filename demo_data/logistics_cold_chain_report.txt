INTRACAPITAL LOGISTICS & COLD CHAIN OPERATIONS REVIEW
Asset Type: HISTORICAL_PROJECT
Asset Name: Cold Chain Perishable Transit Performance Review
Company: Intracapital Corp

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:
During Q1-Q3, Intracapital managed 1,450 refrigerated freight shipments across North American transit corridors. Analysis reveals a 6.8% cargo spoilage rate primarily caused by undetected micro-breaches in container thermal seals during highway transit.

KEY FINDINGS:
1. Spoilage Costs: Over $1.8M lost in damaged vaccines and organic produce.
2. Root Cause: Existing refrigerated trailers lack predictive ambient temperature forecasting and dynamic rerouting tools when ambient heat waves occur.
3. Opportunity Gap: Drivers are only alerted after cargo temperature exceeds critical limits. Real-time predictive thermal early-warning system could reduce spoilage by over 85%.
